Customer care

Questions, considered.

How do I place an order?

Add a product to your bag and prepare an order request at checkout. An order is confirmed only when NIRAVLE accepts it.

How much is delivery?

Shipping fees depend on your address and order. Confirm the exact fee and final total with NIRAVLE before payment.

Can I pay online?

This website does not process payments directly. NIRAVLE accepts Cash on Delivery, bKash and Nagad subject to seller confirmation. Never pay an unverified account.

How do I select size and colour?

Check available product options, then verify size, fabric and colour directly with NIRAVLE before confirming.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Request current return and exchange terms from NIRAVLE before purchasing. Product-specific conditions may apply.

Can I order internationally?

International shipment availability and costs require confirmation from NIRAVLE.
